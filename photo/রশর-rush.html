রাশের Rush

Story & Narrative

A three-panel photographic study capturing the atmosphere of the Rash Utsav rituals. The triptych juxtaposes moments of quiet devotion and hands raised toward a sacred flame against the sharp silhouette of a ceremonial trident. Swirling incense smoke, rich green undertones, and the warm reflection of brass artifacts document the vivid energy of the heritage festival.
